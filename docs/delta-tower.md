# Delta (δ) Tower

Delta is the tier-four cohort tower. Instead of behaving as a conventional gun, it trains a squad of orbiting soldiers that pursue, ram, and damage enemies. Its command strength is a logarithmic transformation of Gamma's total equation.

**Master equation:** `δ = γ × ln(γ + 1)`

## Cohort equations

| Quantity | Equation | Function |
|---|---|---|
| `Atk` | `Atk = γ × ln(γ + 1)` | Command strength and the value returned by Delta's master equation. |
| `Hlth` | `Hlth = γ^ℵ₁` | Maximum health of each Delta soldier. |
| `Spd` | `Spd = 5^ℵ₁` seconds | Training interval used to replenish the cohort. Despite the label, a larger value is a longer interval. |
| `Tot` | `Tot = 3 + ℵ₁` | Maximum number of soldiers in the cohort. |
| `Reg` | `Reg = Hlth / 20` hp/s | Health regenerated by each surviving soldier every second. |
| `Sft` | `Sft = 0.5 × ℵ₂` meters | Backward track displacement applied when a soldier dies while ramming an enemy. |

`ℵ₁` begins at rank 1 and is capped at rank 5 by the blueprint's four upgrade levels. `ℵ₂` begins at zero. Regeneration and shift are supporting mechanics and are not multiplied into the master equation.

**Example:** At `γ = 100` and `ℵ₁ = 2`, `δ = 100 × ln(101) ≈ 461.51`, each soldier has `Hlth = 100² = 10,000`, the training interval is `25 s`, the cohort cap is `5`, and regeneration is `500 hp/s`. At `ℵ₂ = 3`, a fatal ram shifts its target backward `1.5 m`.

## Battlefield function

Delta converts Gamma's ranged power into durable mobile units. Soldiers orbit while idle, can pursue automatically or follow manual targeting orders, regenerate between impacts, and replenish up to the cohort cap. The logarithm tempers Delta's master-equation growth, while the exponent on soldier health makes `ℵ₁` especially influential at high Gamma values.
